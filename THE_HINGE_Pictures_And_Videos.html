The evolution of an era

The Hinge
timeline.

A high-level look at how youth culture moved from broadcast-centered experiences to new forms of digital connection.

CHAPTER 01

2000–2002

Y2K fashion, low-rise jeans, chunky shoes, and the rise of SMS texting shape everyday youth culture. Internet cafés become increasingly visible in urban life.

CHAPTER 02

2003–2006

OPM rock makes waves in mainstream entertainment. Friendster becomes a social ritual, while Asian dramas and local television draw enthusiastic audiences.

CHAPTER 03

2007–2010

Internet cafés, multiplayer games such as DotA and Counter-Strike, and reality talent programs remain important in youth leisure and entertainment. New social platforms gain traction.

EPILOGUE / BEYOND 2010

Today

Digital payments, online marketplaces, social feeds, and networked communities become commonplace. Greater access also brings concerns about privacy and misinformation.
