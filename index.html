BROADCAST / NEW MILLENNIUM

2000 / Pangako Sa ’Yo begins the teleserye era

01 / 11

ABS-CBN's Pangako Sa ’Yo premiered on November 13, 2000, becoming one of the defining teleseryes of the decade. TV remained a major shared cultural space as email and the internet entered everyday conversations.

◎ Original television premiere: 13 Nov 2000

ASIANOVELAS / SMS

2003 / Meteor Garden reaches Philippine screens

04 / 11

The Taiwanese drama Meteor Garden first aired in the Philippines in May 2003, reshaping local pop-cultural tastes. Feature phones and texting were part of the same rapidly changing media landscape.

◎ Philippine broadcast: May 2003
